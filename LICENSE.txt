This repository is a fork.

The original work — Hoshi-no-Tani, "The Valley of Stars": the procedural
terrain, sky and cumulus, wind field, grass, river, trees, viaduct, railway,
village, steam train, post-processing chain and synthesised audio — was created
by Lentils and published on CodePen under the MIT License.

    https://codepen.io/editor/lentils801/pen/019f9b4b-10d7-7f77-817f-f4eb83fdb289

The subsequent work of making that single-file demo portable, maintainable and
extensible by others is by Rob Howard, and is released under the same MIT
License above. It comprises:

  * splitting the 6,184-line single file into 22 ES modules
  * tools/build.py, which reassembles those modules into a single
    self-contained file that still runs from file://
  * replacing an inert `noCast` flag with an enforced scene contract
    (addMesh / addBulk / setDepth), so a mesh can no longer silently
    stop casting a shadow
  * AGENTS.md and README.md
  * the continuous-integration checks and tools/file-protocol-test/
